# 04_quality — risk of bias and the limits of the evidence

**Method:** `../RISK_OF_BIAS.md` (which instrument for which design, and §4 for what depth the ratings reflect).

| Folder | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `appraisal_forms/` | Per-study forms for the studies that got a full signalling-question appraisal (the RoB 2 studies and a number of ROBINS-I and AMSTAR 2 studies), and one **batch note per instrument** (`CASP_Qualitative_batch_…`, `MMAT_batch_…`, `JBI_CrossSectional_batch_…`, `LegalFramework_batch_…`, `ROBINS-I_batch_…`) that records the rule used for all the others |
| `appraisal_forms/A11_…`, `A25_…` | Logs of studies whose instrument was changed after the researcher's rulings |
| `risk_of_bias/` | Notes on the limits of the evidence, dated |

**Who checked it:** nobody yet. Most ratings were assigned by a rule applied to the extracted fields, not by reading each paper against the instrument's signalling questions. One instrument (the Legal Framework appraisal) is this project's own and has not been validated. The rating and tool for every study are in `../03_extraction/extracted_data/extraction_database.csv`.
