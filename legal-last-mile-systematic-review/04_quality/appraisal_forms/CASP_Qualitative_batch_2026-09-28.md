## Source of tool structure

10 items across 3 sections (A: validity, B: results, C: local value) verified directly against
the official CASP Qualitative Checklist (2024) the researcher supplied. Response options: Yes /
No / Can't Tell. **CASP's own guidance explicitly provides no scoring algorithm** — it asks
instead for a qualitative summary (positive/negative/unknown points) and warns: "if there are a
large number of 'Can't tell' responses, consider whether the findings of the study are
trustworthy and interpret the results with caution." No composite score is computed here either,
consistent with that guidance and with today's MMAT batch.

## Methodology (disclosed, rule-based, same approach as prior batches)

- **1 (clear aims):** Yes if `population` is populated (218/227).
- **2 (qualitative methodology appropriate):** Yes if `study_design` explicitly names a
  qualitative approach or method (226/227 — nearly universal, since these studies were tagged
  CASP specifically for being qualitative).
- **3 (research design justified):** Can't tell for all 227 — extraction fields don't capture
  a study's own stated design rationale.
- **4 (recruitment strategy appropriate):** Can't tell for all 227.
- **5 (data collection method addressed the research issue):** Yes only where `study_design`
  names a specific qualitative method (interviews, focus groups, ethnography, documentary/
  archival analysis, narrative research, grounded theory, discourse analysis) rather than just
  the generic label "qualitative case study" — **84 of 227**. The remaining 145 have only a
  generic design label with no named data-collection method, so Can't tell.
- **6 (researcher-participant relationship/reflexivity considered):** Can't tell for all 227 —
  essentially never captured at this project's level of extraction.
- **7 (ethical issues considered):** Can't tell for all 227.
- **8 (data analysis rigor):** Can't tell for all 227.
- **9 (clear statement of findings):** Yes if `effect_estimate` is populated with real findings
  text (204/227 — most studies do have a real extracted finding, since that's the content this
  project's extraction was built to capture).
- **10 (value of the research):** Can't tell for all 227.

**6 of 10 items are "Can't tell" for the entire batch of 227 studies.** Per CASP's own explicit
warning, this is the reader's signal to "interpret the results with caution" — not because these
227 qualitative studies are poorly conducted, but because this project's extraction fields
(built to capture the legal/institutional exposure-outcome content each study contributes, not
its own methodological reporting) never recorded recruitment strategy, reflexivity, ethics
approval, or analytic process for almost any of them. This is the same pattern as today's MMAT
finding, at even larger scale: the largest single batch in this entire risk-of-bias effort is
also the one with the least assessable methodological detail on record.
