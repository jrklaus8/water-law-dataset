# RoB 2 (cluster-randomized trials variant) — S366

Quattrochi et al. 2021, *BMJ Global Health* 6:e005030 — cluster-randomised trial of the Healthy Villages and Schools (VEA) programme, rural DRC;
121 clusters (50 intervention / 71 control), 1,312 households in 328 villages. Same trial as S294 (LR01).

**Basis for this appraisal (2026-09-29): the full-text PDF supplied by the researcher** (11 pp; not committed). This replaces the abstract-only,
low-confidence appraisal of 2026-09-28. **Not read:** Figure 1 (trial profile, an image) and the online supplemental tables. Answers below are
those the paper text supports; where it is silent the answer is NI, not assumed. Appraised by the AI; not independently verified.

| Domain | Key signalling answers (from the text) | Judgement | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1a. Randomization process | 1a.1 Y (Stata, stratified by province and number of villages per cluster) · 1a.2 PY (all clusters allocated in one step from a list drawn up before implementation; no concealment statement) · 1a.3 N (Table 1: household characteristics balanced) | **Low** | Caveat: "seven villages were randomly dropped" to meet operational targets; timing relative to allocation not stated |

Identification/recruitment timing | Households were selected at endline only, by interviewers blinded to assignment, walking from the village centre to a randomly chosen nth household (n = 1–20); four per village | **Low** | No post-allocation selection by people who knew the arm; note one questionnaire module covered programme participation (a possible unblinding route, assessed under Domain 4) |

**Overall judgement: Some concerns** for the primary outcomes (improved water source +33 pp, 95% CI 22 to 45; improved sanitation +26 pp,
95% CI 14 to 37). The satisfaction/behaviour/governance indices would be **High** on Domain 4 alone. This is now an evidence-based
"Some concerns", not the placeholder of 2026-09-28: no domain reaches High for the primary outcomes, and the concerns are named.

**Still to verify** (needs the trial registry entry, Figure 1 and the supplement): registration timing relative to data collection; why 328 of 332
villages appear; household-level non-response; how many intervention villages completed the programme.

**Relation to S294:** same 121 clusters and 332 villages, but S294 (2025) reports 3,283 households; this paper's 1,312 households (four per village,
Oct–Dec 2019) come from a different, smaller survey. The two are one trial, not necessarily one dataset; treat as one trial in any count.
