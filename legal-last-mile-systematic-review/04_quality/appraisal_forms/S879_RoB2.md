# RoB 2 (cluster-randomized trials variant) — S879

Coville, Galiani, Gertler & Yoshida 2025, *Review of Economics and Statistics*
(NBER WP 27569) — engagement vs. systematic contract-enforcement (disconnection
notices) arms, randomized field experiment, Nairobi informal settlements.

**Randomization-unit caveat (carried from `risk_of_bias_tool`):** this study is
appraised here under the cluster-trial variant because the recorded intervention
unit is "96 disconnected compounds" / "engagement and enforcement arms" —
compound-level, not household-level — but the extraction record never states
"cluster-randomized" explicitly. **If the source paper confirms individual/
household-level randomization instead, this entire appraisal needs to be redone
against the individually-randomized parallel-trial instrument, not this one** —
the two forms ask materially different questions (this one's Domain 1b,
identification/recruitment timing, would not apply at all).

**Basis for this appraisal:** `extraction_database.csv` fields, Tables 4–7 cited.
Positive signal not available for the other four studies in this batch: the
`regulatory_model` field records the trial's **AEA RCT Registry ID
(AEARCTR-0003556)** — i.e. this trial was pre-registered, a real, specific,
non-inferred fact.
