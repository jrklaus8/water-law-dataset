## Overall confidence rating: Not ratable, but flagged as the strongest case in the corpus

Of the 5 applicable critical items (2, 4, 7, 9, 13): **3 have real, extracted evidence — all
Partial Yes (4, 9, 13)**; 2 remain NI (2, 7). This is the only AMSTAR 2-eligible study in the
corpus where a majority of applicable critical items have a positive, evidenced answer
rather than "no information." A defensible formal label still cannot be assigned — item 2
(protocol registration) in particular is a real open question, and many realist reviews are
not formally pre-registered, so this could go either way. **Even if items 2 and 7 resolve
favourably, the Partial-Yes (not full Yes) answers on 4, 9, and 13 would still cap this
review's confidence at Low by AMSTAR 2's own algorithm** — worth stating plainly so a future
full-text re-extraction isn't mistaken for a path to a High or Moderate rating.
