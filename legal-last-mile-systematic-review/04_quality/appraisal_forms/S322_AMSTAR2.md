## Source of tool structure

Tool structure (all 16 items, verbatim/near-verbatim wording, the 7 critical items -- 2, 4, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15 -- and the High/Moderate/Low/Critically-Low confidence-rating algorithm) obtained via WebSearch on 2026-09-16 and cross-checked across multiple independent search results (Wiley/PMC/ScienceDirect summaries of Shea et al. 2017's AMSTAR 2 publication). WebFetch to amstar.ca and every publisher domain tried returned EGRESS_BLOCKED in this environment (see CHANGELOG.md 2026-09-16), so this is reconstructed from search snippets rather than the primary document -- unlike the RoB 2/ROBINS-I pilot batch, the tool text itself is reasonably complete here. **The limiting factor for this batch is not tool completeness but study-extraction depth**: every one of these 17 studies was extracted from a citation, DOI, and a one-to-few-sentence abstract/effect_estimate summary -- not from the review's own full Methods section. AMSTAR 2 appraises what a review's report actually describes (protocol registration, duplicate screening, search databases, funding disclosure, etc.); not knowing whether a review describes something is different from confirming it does not, so per researcher decision this batch marks an item 'Not assessable' rather than scoring it 'No' whenever the extracted text is silent on it, even though AMSTAR 2's own convention would often score an unaddressed item as a straightforward 'No'. This is a deliberately more conservative choice than the tool's own default, made to avoid penalizing a review for silence in an abstract that its full paper may not share.

## Overall confidence rating: Not ratable

AMSTAR 2's overall rating (High/Moderate/Low/Critically Low) is derived from a defined combination of all 7 critical items (2, 4, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15) plus the 9 non-critical items -- any single critical-item weakness caps the rating at Low, and more than one caps it at Critically Low. Of this study's 7 critical items, 7 have no extracted evidence at all (marked 'Not assessable' above), so the algorithm's precondition (a defensible answer to every critical item) is not met. Assigning any of the four rating labels here would require guessing at unassessed critical items, which this appraisal does not do. **A real AMSTAR 2 rating for this study requires retrieving and extracting its full Methods section, not merely the abstract/citation-level detail available in this extraction pass.**
