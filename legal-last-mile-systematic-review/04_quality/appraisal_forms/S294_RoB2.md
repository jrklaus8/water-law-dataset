## Domain-level judgements

| Domain | Judgement | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Bias arising from the randomization process | Some concerns | Cluster-randomized controlled trial design confirmed. Allocation-concealment procedure and formal baseline-balance check are not described in the extracted text. |
| 2. Bias due to deviations from intended interventions | Some concerns | Intention-to-treat analysis explicitly stated, appropriate for a community-level intervention that cannot be blinded. No information on the extent or handling of any deviations from the intended intervention. |

Bias in measurement of the outcome | Mixed by outcome -- see note | Length-for-age Z-score is an objective anthropometric measurement, unlikely to differ in ascertainment between arms (lower risk). The community-WASH-institutions index is survey/assessor-based and similarly not immune to differential ascertainment (some concerns). |
| 5. Bias in selection of the reported result | Not assessable -- incomplete tool text obtained in this environment | No information on a pre-specified analysis plan or outcome/analysis selection was extracted from this study's text. |
