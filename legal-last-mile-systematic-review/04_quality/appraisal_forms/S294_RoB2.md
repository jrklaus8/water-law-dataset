# RoB 2 (cluster-randomized trials variant) — S294

Quattrochi et al. 2025, *PLOS Medicine* — community-driven WASH intervention with
institutional-strengthening component vs. control, cluster RCT, rural DRC
(121 clusters, 332 villages, 3,283 households).

**Basis for this appraisal:** `extraction_database.csv` fields only; no page/table/
figure locators were recorded at extraction time (unlike S085), so this appraisal
is thinner than S085's. Completes the 2026-09-16 partial pilot, which left Domains
3 and 5 "not assessable."
