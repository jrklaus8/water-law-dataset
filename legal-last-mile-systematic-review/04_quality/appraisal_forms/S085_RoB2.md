## Source of tool structure

Tool structure obtained via WebSearch (WebFetch to every publisher/repository domain tried -- unisa.edu.au, cochrane.de, methods.cochrane.org, training.cochrane.org, corates.org, arxiv.org, strataresearch.net -- returned EGRESS_BLOCKED in this environment; see CHANGELOG.md 2026-09-16). Domains 3 (missing outcome data) and 5 (selection of the reported result) could not be recovered as specific numbered signalling questions -- only their general purpose was confirmed -- so per researcher decision 2026-09-16 they are judged only where the extracted study text itself documents something squarely on-topic (e.g. a stated attrition rate, a stated pre-registration), and marked 'not assessable -- incomplete tool text obtained in this environment' otherwise. This is a partial, flagged appraisal, not a complete RoB 2 application -- do not treat the overall rating below as equivalent to one produced from the full official tool.

## Domain-level judgements

| Domain | Judgement | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Bias arising from the randomization process | Some concerns | 2x2 factorial, block-randomized, staggered-rollout cluster RCT confirmed (genuine randomization affirmed). Allocation-concealment procedure is not described in the extracted text -- no information on signalling question 1.2. |
| 2. Bias due to deviations from intended interventions | Some concerns | Main specification is unadjusted (block fixed effects only), consistent with an intent-to-treat framing. Participants cannot be blinded to receiving bureaucratic-assistance/political-coordination treatment by the nature of the intervention -- expected for this design, but no information was extracted on how any resulting deviations were monitored or handled. |
| 3. Bias due to missing outcome data | Some concerns | Real extracted evidence bears directly on this domain even without the exact numbered signalling questions: 61.5% endline recontact rate is a substantial (though not extreme) attrition level, but balance tests reported in the study's own Online Appendix M show no evidence of asymmetric attrition by treatment condition -- i.e. missingness does not appear informative/differential by arm, which is the concern this domain targets. |
| 4. Bias in measurement of the outcome | Some concerns | Outcome is 'full formalization' administrative status. The extracted text does not state whether this was verified via official municipal/land-title records or self-report, so differential ascertainment between arms cannot be ruled out. |
| 5. Bias in selection of the reported result | Low | Real extracted evidence bears directly on this domain: a pre-registered analysis plan (with one filed amendment) is reported, with primary hypotheses evaluated at nominal p-values and secondary hypotheses corrected for multiple comparisons (Benjamini-Hochberg) -- exactly what this domain is designed to check for. |

## Overall judgement: Some concerns (partial)

No assessed domain shows 'High' or worse; Domains 1, 2, and 4 show 'Some concerns' from missing procedural detail, Domain 3 shows 'Some concerns' from genuine attrition evidence, and Domain 5 is 'Low' on genuine pre-registration evidence. This is the most completely assessable of the three RoB 2 studies in this pilot batch, though still not equivalent to a full official-tool application.
