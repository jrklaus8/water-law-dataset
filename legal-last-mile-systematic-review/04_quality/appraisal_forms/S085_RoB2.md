# RoB 2 (cluster-randomized trials variant) — S085

Gaikwad & Thomas 2026, *American Journal of Political Science* — bureaucratic
assistance (T1) × political coordination (T2), 2×2 factorial, block-randomized,
staggered-rollout cluster RCT, Mumbai informal settlements.

**Basis for this appraisal:** `extraction_database.csv` fields, extracted from the
full published paper (Tables 1–2, Figures 1/6/7/9, pp. 1–17), a richer source than
S057/S294/S366 below. Several signalling questions still land on NI where the
extracted fields don't cover the specific point (e.g. the exact sequence-generation
mechanism), but this study has the most complete evidentiary basis of the five.

Supersedes/completes the 2026-09-16 partial pilot appraisal (which already had
real, non-"not assessable" judgements for 4 of 5 generic domains) by re-mapping
onto the correct cluster-specific structure, which adds Domain 1b — identification/
recruitment timing — not present in the simpler 5-domain framework used in the
2026-09-16 pass.

| Domain | Key signalling answers | Judgement | Rationale |

Randomization process | 1a.1 PY (explicit "block-randomized" design) · 1a.2 NI (concealment procedure not in extracted text) · 1a.3 PN (block fixed effects in the main specification structurally control for block-level baseline differences, a positive but indirect signal) | **Some concerns** | NI on concealment keeps this from Low risk despite the favourable 1a.3 signal |

**Overall judgement: Some concerns.** The strongest-designed study in this batch
(pre-registered, block-randomized, factorial), but concealment procedure and
outcome-ascertainment method are not documented in what was extracted, and
attrition — while balance-tested — is real. No domain reaches High risk.
Consistent with, and completes, the 2026-09-16 partial rating.
