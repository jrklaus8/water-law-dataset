**Eligibility against the criteria (AI judgement).**

1. *Water or sanitation service access:* yes (WASH access of people experiencing homelessness).
2. *Legal, administrative or governance factor:* yes, strongly: legal recognition without operational mechanisms, decentralised decrees, coordination and funding gaps, urban rules that criminalise public-space use (Neves-Silva & Heller 2016, cited).
3. *Empirical evidence or systematic empirical synthesis:* **borderline.** It states a search, criteria and a flow diagram and analyses documents, so it is an evidence synthesis of a kind, but there is no protocol or registration, no duplicate screening or extraction described, no appraisal of the sources and no list of excluded documents; the corpus is two-thirds grey and legal documents.
4. *Outcome relevant to access, availability or exclusion:* partly: qualitative conclusions on unequal and undignified access; no measured access outcome and no effect estimate.
5-7. Population, exposure and design are identifiable (documentary integrative review); criterion 6 (effect estimate) does not apply.
