# Controlled vocabulary for `mechanism_family` and `outcome_family` (drafted 2026-09-28, adopted 2026-10-14)

**Status: ADOPTED by the PI on 2026-10-14 (open item A5).** `evidence_map.csv` now carries the single-label family below in both columns; the original free-text labels are preserved in
`evidence_map_original_labels_2026-10-14.csv`. Generated by `code/analysis/propose_family_vocabulary.py` (deterministic; every rule is in that file). The mapping is mechanical and
keyword-based, not a validated coding; the AI that produced the original labels also produced it, so a human should still spot-check a sample. 37 rows the keyword rules could not place, or placed doubtfully, were resolved by the AI on 2026-10-14 in
`family_vocabulary_overrides_2026-10-14.csv` (one-line basis each; the PI can veto any of them). The studies still `unmapped_needs_review` are the genuine edge cases listed at the end.

## The problem

`mechanism_family` holds 231 distinct labels and `outcome_family` 123 across 1,104 studies. The documented enum
(`DATA_DICTIONARY.md`) has 5 and 4 values; in practice the fields mix the enum, upper- and lower-case variants, comma-separated
lists and one-off free-text descriptions. Any tabulation by family is therefore unreliable as it stands.

## Proposed vocabulary

### Mechanism families (multi-label allowed)

| Family | Meaning | Extraction booleans that map to it |
|---|---|---|
| `eligibility_status_documentation` | Who qualifies: legal recognition, tenure/property status, required documents | `eligibility`, `documentation`, `tenure`, `property` |
| `procedural_burden` | Cost of getting through the process: steps, delay, permits | `burden`, `procedural_steps`, `delay`, `building_permit` |
| `fees_tariffs_subsidies` | Money rules: connection fees, tariff design, subsidies, price regulation | `fees` |
| `discretion_accommodation` | Official discretion, hardship exceptions and accommodation | `discretion_accommodation`, `discretion`, `hardship_exception` |
| `enforcement_sanctions` | Enforcement, disconnection, reconnection, sanctions | `enforcement`, `disconnection`, `reconnection`, `sanction` |
| `review_redress` | Administrative review, complaints, judicial review | `administrative_review`, `complaint`, `judicial_review` |
| `institutional_structure_coordination` | Fragmented or decentralised institutions, service-area and planning boundaries, political coordination | `institutional_fragmentation`, `political_coordination`, `service_area`, `planning`, `zoning` |
| `participation_assistance` | Community participation and bureaucratic assistance to applicants | `participation`, `bureaucratic_assistance` |
| `regulatory_model_ownership` | Who owns/runs/regulates the service: regulatory model, ownership, concession, reform | (label keywords only — no boolean exists) |

Studies with more than one family are `multiple` in the single-label column and list every family in `mechanism_families_all`.

### Outcome families

The four already documented in `PROJECT_SPEC.md` §7 / `DATA_DICTIONARY.md`: `primary_connection` (formal connection),
`effective_access` (quantity, reliability, continuity, quality, coverage, water/sanitation access), `economic_access`
(cost, affordability, tariff burden) and `administrative_outcome` (approval, refusal, delay, appeal, complaint). Labels such as
`water_access`, `sanitation_access`, `service_coverage`, `affordability` and `formal_connection` are treated as refinements
and folded into these four; `PROJECT_SPEC.md` §7 warns not to pool them merely because they share the word "access".

## Known weaknesses of this draft

- Keyword rules can mis-assign a one-off label (e.g. a label containing "community" is sent to `participation_assistance`).
- `multiple` is large because 377 studies already carry the label `MULTIPLE` (more than one of the four top-level booleans TRUE) and specific labels that name several families also land there; use `mechanism_families_all` for breadth.
- The multi-label counts are broad because the extraction booleans are TRUE for most studies (e.g. institutional fragmentation for about three quarters); they show low discriminating power of the booleans — itself a finding for the taxonomy decision, not a clean count of "studies about X".
- Extraction booleans are sparse-to-noisy (blank is not FALSE), so a study with few TRUE booleans may be under-labelled.
- `effective_access` is broad by design (it absorbs coverage, reliability, quantity, quality, continuity); a finer split may be wanted.
- No inter-rater check exists. The PI adopted the vocabulary on 2026-10-14 (`00_admin/DECISIONS_AND_OPEN_ITEMS.md` A5) knowing this.

## Edge cases still `unmapped_needs_review`

These studies' recorded mechanism is a broad determinant or an external macro-policy, not a legal or administrative rule that fits one of the nine families; they keep the value `unmapped_needs_review` rather than a forced label.

- S1056: 'social_capital_caste' (outcome: effective_access)
- S1065: 'regional_disparity' (outcome: effective_access)
- S1072: 'IMF structural adjustment conditionality' (outcome: effective_access)
- S1079: 'broad socioeconomic/structural determinants (investment, agricultural share, vulnerable female employment, education, ur' (outcome: effective_access)
- S1084: 'broad socioeconomic/geographic determinants (age, education, wealth, distance, region)' (outcome: effective_access)
