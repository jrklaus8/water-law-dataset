# Draft mapping of free-text study design labels to the 8-value enum

Generated by `code/analysis/propose_design_vocabulary.py`. 349 of 1,159 studies in `evidence_map.csv` carry a free-text `study_design_class` (the Legal Institutional Evidence Appraisal Framework and no-tool studies; see `DATA_DICTIONARY.md` and `study_design_class_normalization_2026-09-28.md`). This is a **proposal only**: nothing in `evidence_map.csv` changes, and the keyword rules have no ground truth.

| Proposed class | Studies |
|---|---|
| qualitative | 286 |
| mixed_methods | 31 |
| doctrinal | 13 |
| unmapped_needs_review | 10 |
| observational | 4 |
| quasi_experimental | 3 |
| systematic_review_secondary | 2 |

How to read it: the enum has no value for what most of these studies are (institutional, historical, documentary or ethnographic case studies). The `qualitative` bucket therefore lumps together designs `RISK_OF_BIAS.md` section 2 treats as different; `doctrinal` catches only legal-only labels; `unmapped_needs_review` are essays, theory or models. If the researcher wants the field fully normalised, the choice is between (a) accept this mapping, (b) extend the enum with a value such as `qualitative_institutional_case_study`, or (c) leave the free text (current state). The rules are in the script's docstring; each row of the CSV names the rule that fired.
