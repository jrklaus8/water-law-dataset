# Mapping of free-text study design labels to the 8-value enum (adopted 2026-10-14)

Generated by `code/analysis/propose_design_vocabulary.py`. 338 of 1,104 studies in `evidence_map.csv` carried a free-text `study_design_class` (the Legal Institutional Evidence Appraisal Framework and no-tool studies; see `DATA_DICTIONARY.md` and `study_design_class_normalization_2026-09-28.md`). **Adopted by the PI on 2026-10-14 (open item A20, option a):** `evidence_map.csv` holds the mapped class for the 328 studies the rules could map; the 10 `unmapped_needs_review` studies keep their free-text label. The original strings are kept in `evidence_map_original_labels_2026-10-14.csv` (and in the `free_text_label` column of the mapping CSV). The keyword rules have no ground truth, so the mapping is mechanical and unvalidated; the `qualitative` class lumps designs `RISK_OF_BIAS.md` section 2 treats as different (ethnographic, historical, institutional and documentary case studies).

| Proposed class | Studies |
|---|---|
| qualitative | 275 |
| mixed_methods | 31 |
| doctrinal | 13 |
| unmapped_needs_review | 10 |
| observational | 4 |
| quasi_experimental | 3 |
| systematic_review_secondary | 2 |

How to read it: the enum has no value for what most of these studies are (institutional, historical, documentary or ethnographic case studies). The `qualitative` bucket therefore lumps together designs `RISK_OF_BIAS.md` section 2 treats as different; `doctrinal` catches only legal-only labels; `unmapped_needs_review` are essays, theory or models. The PI chose option (a), accept this mapping; extending the enum (for example a `qualitative_institutional_case_study` value) remains possible later, and the preserved originals make that cheap. The rules are in the script's docstring; each row of the CSV names the rule that fired.
