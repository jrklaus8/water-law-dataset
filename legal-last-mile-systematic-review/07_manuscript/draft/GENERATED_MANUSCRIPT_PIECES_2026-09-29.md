## 3. Limitations (draft)

- **Incomplete full-text screening.** 1,382 of 3,659 records (37.8%) were not assessed, and those not assessed are older on average; the review is not exhaustive and may under-represent earlier literature.
- **AI-conducted judgments with partial verification.** Screening decisions, extraction, appraisal and coding of direction of association were made by the same AI system; only 197 full-text includes were human-confirmed, 73 decided rows had no reviewer recorded at the time (the label was inferred afterwards), and the title/abstract exclusions were not human-checked. A blind re-read by two non-Claude models (Codex, Gemini) of 70 of the AI's full-text excludes (a stratified sample, limited to records whose PDF was available) judged 34 of them to be includes; this is an AI-versus-AI check, it was not human-adjudicated, and, scaled by exclusion code, the rate would imply roughly 532 further includes if the models were right, and an AI triage of the same records puts about 37 under a narrow reading of the criteria (arithmetic only), so the exclusion log may understate the eligible literature substantially; in the other direction, the models would exclude 3 of 40 sampled unconfirmed includes, roughly 66 of the unconfirmed includes if the rate held (open item A16; `02_screening/full_text/A16_ADJUDICATION_SHEET_2026-10-04.md`).
- **Thin extraction for some studies.** 10 studies were extracted from abstract or metadata only; there was no second extractor. The AI re-read 127 studies from full texts after the first extraction (an AI check of AI work, not independent verification); in 9 of the 76 sparse-audit rows among them the abstract-level extraction was materially wrong or incomplete (`CAMPAIGN_NOTES.md`).
- **Appraisal validity.** Ratings are rule-based; 426 studies were appraised with a non-validated project instrument; 51 of 130 JBI ratings are 'high concern' largely reflecting sparse extraction; only 18 of 18 AMSTAR 2 reviews could be given a confidence rating (17 Critically Low and 1 Low).
- **No pooled estimate.** Direction-of-association syntheses rest on 59 effect-size rows and are neither effect estimates nor certainty-graded (GRADE).
- **Classifications.** Mechanism, outcome and study-design labels were mapped to controlled vocabularies by keyword rules on 2026-10-14 (original labels preserved); the mapping is mechanical and unvalidated, and country and legal-system fields remain free text.
- **A post-hoc scope reading.** Eligibility criterion 2 was applied under a narrow, rule-type reading adopted on 2026-10-13, after an AI-versus-AI disagreement about the exclusions had been seen, and followed by consistency passes (open items A16 and A22 to A25) that moved 83 included reports to the exclusions and three exclusions to the inclusions. It is a post-hoc clarification, not an a priori rule; the wording of the criterion is unchanged, and a different reading would change the included set (`INCLUSION_EXCLUSION.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`).
- **Counts are of reports.** The 1,080 included items are reports: they describe 1,078 distinct studies on the definite same-data links (1,076 counting the partial overlaps), the five RoB 2 report rows are four trials, and S526 and S539 (two US utility studies) are flagged for verification of sample overlap before any pooling.
- **Search scope.** SSRN and Westlaw/Lexis were not searched (`README.md`, Known limitations).
