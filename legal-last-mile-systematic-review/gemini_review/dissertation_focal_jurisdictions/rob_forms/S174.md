## 3. Overall Narrative Summary

This analytical cross-sectional study examines the association between municipal institutional capacity (financial, technical, operational and political factor scores derived from 2018 SNIS and 2017 IBGE indicators) and water, sewerage and solid-waste coverage across municipalities in Minas Gerais, Brazil. Appraised with the JBI Critical Appraisal Checklist for Analytical Cross Sectional Studies, the study raises High concern for risk of bias (2 Yes, 4 Unclear, 2 No). Key threats to internal and external validity include: (1) severe selection and missing-data bias, as undocumented boxplot outlier removal reduced the sample to 574 municipalities and missing SNIS/IBGE data (especially non-reported municipal sanitation plans and policies) further restricted the regression sample to N = 391; (2) unadjusted confounding and simultaneity/reverse causality, as the OLS regressions omit all structural municipal confounders (such as population size, urbanization, income/HDI and service-provider type) while using endogenous per-capita sanitation revenues/expenditures and reactive plan adoption to predict contemporaneous coverage; (3) use of Pearson-correlation PCA on binary legal/technical indicators; and (4) self-reported SNIS administrative measurements whose completeness and accuracy depend on municipal institutional capacity itself.
