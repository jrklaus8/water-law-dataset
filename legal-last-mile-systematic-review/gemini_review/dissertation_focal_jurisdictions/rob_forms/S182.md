## 3. Overall Narrative Summary

Study S182 (Lima et al., 2022) is a quantitative cross-sectional household perception survey (n=393 questionnaires across 6,573 households) in the Santa Rosa neighborhood of Guarujá, São Paulo, stratified into 7 census-sector groups via an adapted 2010 IBGE Census Social Development Index (SDI) and visualized with GIS alongside doctrinal discussion of municipal sanitation, solid waste, and macro-drainage laws/plans. Appraised with the JBI Critical Appraisal Checklist for Analytical Cross Sectional Studies, the study raises High concern overall (5 Unclear items: Q1-Q5; 3 No items: Q6-Q8). Key strengths include transparent census-tract SDI mapping, full reproduction of the survey instrument in Appendix I, and integration of local planning legislation (Complementary Laws 108/2007, 227/2018, and 228/2018; Ordinary Law 4,367/2016) to contextualize irregular-settlement service gaps. However, internal validity and precision are substantially limited by: (1) omission of survey fieldwork dates, within-household respondent eligibility rules, refusal rates, and respondent demographics; (2) an adapted 2010 SDI missing two schooling indicators without weight recalibration and lagging behind survey administration; (3) an unvalidated 9-item questionnaire using forced binary quality ratings ('Adequate' vs 'Precarious'), a double-barreled waste/cleaning item (Question 5), and unspecified plan wording (Question 8); (4) absence of individual-level confounder measurement or statistical adjustment; and (5) purely descriptive, unweighted percentage analyses based on a 12% per-group error margin, unmodeled block clustering, sharply unequal group sampling fractions, and numerical discrepancies between text (Page 13) and Table 4 (Page 18).
