## 3. Overall Narrative Summary

Maiello, de Paiva Britto, and Quintslr (2021) present a convergent/triangulated mixed-methods case study of formal-informal hybrid water supply ('the spotted zebra') in Queimados, Rio de Janeiro Metropolitan Region. Appraised with MMAT (2018) using the Quantitative Descriptive block (4.1–4.5), the study's main strengths lie in its conceptual coherence and genuine mixed-methods integration (5.1–5.4 all Yes): the authors align a 90-respondent user survey (analysed with Multiple Correspondence Analysis) and 9 semi-structured stakeholder interviews (inductively coded following Thomas 2006) with Faldi et al.'s (2019) area-actors-flows framework, and effectively contrast subjective user perceptions (100% rating spring water good-to-excellent) against institutional testimonies and microbiological records showing Escherichia coli contamination and spring depletion.
