## 3. Overall Narrative Summary

Roth (2008) presents a ten-year qualitative anthropological case study of the struggle by 64 well-dependent households on Senanus Drive (Central Saanich, British Columbia, Canada) to obtain a 4 km municipal watermain extension. Appraised with the CASP Qualitative Checklist, the study exhibits clear aims, an appropriate longitudinal ethnographic/STS case-study design, rich multi-source documentary and meeting-transcript triangulation, and clear substantive findings on how municipal bylaws (RE-3 zoning, Bylaw No. 1410, petition thresholds), anti-development politics, and conflicting groundwater reports constrained safe-water access (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q5, Q9 = Yes).
