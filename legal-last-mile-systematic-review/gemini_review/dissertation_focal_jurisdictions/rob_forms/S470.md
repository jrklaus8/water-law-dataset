## 3. Overall Narrative Summary

Amorim et al. (2025) conduct a non-randomized before-and-after longitudinal panel evaluation of regulatory rulemaking and enforcement by Minas Gerais's state water regulator (Arsae-MG) on municipal implementation of the water and sanitation social tariff across 572 Copasa-MG municipalities (2020-2021 main balanced panel; 2018-2021 robustness panel). Appraised with ROBINS-I, the overall risk of bias is Serious, driven by Domain 1 (confounding): because the regulatory norm (Regulation No. 150/2021) and economic inspections applied simultaneously across all Copasa-MG municipalities and no external untreated comparison group existed, the treatment variable (REGU = 1 in 2021) is an uncontrolled pre-post period dummy that is collinear with concurrent statewide shocks, including State Law No. 23,670 (July 2020) and recovery from a May 2020 COVID-19 dip in registration (mean DIST was 76.1% in 2018 and 78.3% in 2019, fell to 68.3% in May 2020, and rose to 87.5% in July 2021). Selection bias (D2) and missing-data bias (D5) are Low due to virtually complete administrative coverage of single-provider Copasa-MG municipalities (572/572 in 2020-2021; 570/572 in 2018-2021). Intervention classification (D3) and outcome measurement (D6) carry Moderate risk because REGU bundles statutory, inspection, and regulatory changes together, and the aggregate DIST Index is right-censored at 100% and lacks household-level cross-checking against billing errors. Selection of the reported result (D7) is rated No information due to the absence of a pre-analysis plan, alongside disclosed institutional authorship at Arsae-MG.
