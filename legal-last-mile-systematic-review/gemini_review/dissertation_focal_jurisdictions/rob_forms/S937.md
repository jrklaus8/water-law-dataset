## 3. Overall Narrative Summary

Structured non-validated appraisal using the Legal Institutional Evidence Appraisal Framework: de Carvalho et al. (2019) present an ex-ante Regulatory Impact Assessment (RIA) case study of household wastewater connection policy in four municipalities of Minas Gerais, Brazil (Belo Horizonte, Betim, Contagem, and Ribeirao das Neves), regulated by ARSAE. The study exhibits strong legal source accuracy, jurisdictional specificity, and institutional contextualization—accurately detailing Federal Law no. 11445/2007 (art. 45), State Law no. 13317/1999 (art. 48), and State Law no. 18309/2009, and explaining why ARSAE cannot directly inspect or sanction non-connected households (a municipal power) and must instead act via tariff incentives. However, as empirical evidence on regulatory effectiveness, the study has substantial methodological limitations: all policy impacts (75% connection-gap reduction, BRL 33 million expenditure, BRL 42 million revenue surplus, 17,414 tons less sludge, and 12.5% waterborne disease avoidance) are ex-ante projections rather than observed ex-post outcomes; baseline linear projections (apparently using very few observations, given 2 degrees of freedom in Equation 3) omit household and municipal confounders; focus-group, conference-panel, and web-survey sample sizes and selection procedures are unreported (and exclude environmental/water-resource entities); co-author affiliation with ARSAE is not reflexively examined in relation to preference weighting; and core data, value functions, and cost-benefit derivations are deferred to online appendices alongside minor timeframe and option-label inconsistencies in the main text.
