## 3. Overall Narrative Summary

Badri and Joshi (2018) present an empirical impact assessment of Shelter Associates' 'One Home-One Toilet' (OHOT) urban slum household sanitation delivery model implemented in partnership with Urban Local Bodies across four Maharashtra cities (Kolhapur, Navi-Mumbai, Pimpri-Chinchwad, and Pune). The primary empirical component is an internal two-wave pre-post household survey of 463 beneficiary families interviewed 6 months apart (June 2016-June 2018), supplemented by six headline summary bullets from an external 1-year 'pre-post case-control study' of 386 households conducted by the Gokhale Institute of Politics & Economics. Appraised with ROBINS-I, the study is at Critical overall risk of bias. First, the primary 463-household evaluation lacks an untreated concurrent control group and performs zero statistical adjustment for household socioeconomic confounders or secular trends (such as concurrent Swachh Bharat Mission campaigns), while omitting all control-selection and adjustment details for the external 386-household study (D1: Critical). Second, sampling an unspecified 15% subset solely among OHOT cost-sharing adopters introduces severe selection bias when drawing conclusions about slum households' general readiness to construct toilets or willingness to share costs (D2: Serious). Third, unblinded data collection by the implementing NGO's own field team, interviewing only female household members (proxy-reporting male open defecation/CTB use) and relying on unvalidated self-report for UTI symptoms and program satisfaction, creates Serious outcome measurement bias (D6: Serious). Finally, unexplained respondent count discrepancies (2,414 pre-intervention vs. 2,306 post-intervention in the Abstract vs. 2,263 in Table 1, plus a 90.0% column sum in Table 3; D5: Moderate), concurrent housing upgrades by 52.5% of households (D4: Moderate), and selective narrative reporting of most stated outcome domains without test statistics or protocols (D7: Serious) further limit causal inference.
