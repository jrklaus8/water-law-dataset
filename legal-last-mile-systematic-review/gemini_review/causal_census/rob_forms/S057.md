## 3. Overall Narrative Summary

Study S057 (Kozole et al., 2023) is a 30-village cluster-randomized controlled trial in Kampong Chhnang province, rural Cambodia (2,821 unique household sales presentations), supplemented by 13 stakeholder interviews and a programmatic cost analysis, evaluating whether a climate- and poverty-targeted 50% price subsidy (combining Cambodia's IDPoor registry, commune Climate Vulnerability Index [CVI], and household Poverty Probability Index [PPI] plus self-reported climate shocks) increases sales conversion for resilient latrines. Appraised with the RoB 2 cluster-randomized trials variant, the study has an overall High risk of bias. While village-level randomization was properly executed via Stata's randtreat stratified on commune climate vulnerability with baseline balance checks (D1a: Low), household identification and recruitment occurred post-randomization through unblinded sales agents and village authorities (D1b: High). As confirmed by both administrative data and qualitative agent interviews, village authorities in treatment clusters provided greater support in locating poor, unlatrined households, producing a statistically significant excess of subsidy-eligible (51–52% vs. 45%, p = 0.00) and IDPoor (+5 percentage points, p = 0.00) households in treatment villages. Additional Some concerns arise across D2–D5: unblinded implementation involved 1,598 repeat visits that were dropped post hoc alongside persistent agent misunderstandings of PPI scoring rules (D2); post-order follow-up was truncated before installation, omitting typical 10–20% order cancellation rates and unreported household non-contact/refusal denominators (D3); outcomes captured unblinded agent-recorded latrine orders rather than verified installations, with M&E spot-checks restricted to ~10% of buyers and subjective self-reported household climate shocks (D4); and no trial registration or pre-analysis plan was reported alongside sparse main-text statistical reporting and minor numerical inconsistencies across sections (D5).
