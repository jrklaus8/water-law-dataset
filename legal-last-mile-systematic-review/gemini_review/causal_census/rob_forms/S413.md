## 3. Overall Narrative Summary

Oliveira and Lima (2015) conduct a convergent mixed-methods empirical evaluation of the 2006 stock-market initial public offering (IPO) of Minas Gerais's state water and sanitation utility (COPASA), combining descriptive and non-parametric statistical analyses of official and corporate datasets (IBGE PNSB 2000 and 2008, Demographic Census 2000 and 2010, Central Bank inflation indices, and COPASA reports 2003-2012) with content and discourse analysis of 15 semi-structured interviews with sector managers, regulators, technicians, and specialists. Appraised with the Mixed Methods Appraisal Tool (MMAT 2018; Qualitative 1.1-1.5, Quantitative non-randomized 3.1-3.5, and Mixed-Methods 5.1-5.5), the study demonstrates strong qualitative coherence and thoughtful mixed-methods integration: qualitative evidence is systematically interwoven with indicator trends to explain institutional incentives, tariff pass-throughs, outsourcing problems, and why observed coverage changes cannot be naively attributed to the IPO. However, per MMAT 2018 guidance that overall quality cannot exceed the weakest component, significant methodological limitations arise in the quantitative non-randomized component (3.1, 3.2, 3.4, 3.5 rated No; 3.3 Can't tell, noting also that Tables 1-12 and Figures 1-8 are embedded as unextracted raster images in the PDF): (1) comparing 2000 vs 2008/2010 outcomes conflates six pre-IPO years with only two-to-four post-IPO years; (2) restricting longitudinal Mann-Whitney U tests to municipalities continuously and exclusively operated by COPASA (n = 64) or municipal entities (n = 256) excludes over half the state's municipalities, including mixed-service systems and post-2006 expansion municipalities; (3) PNSB protocol changes between 2000 and 2008 impaired sewage comparability and continuity/intermittence metrics were unavailable; and (4) bivariate non-parametric tests make no statistical adjustment for baseline ceiling effects or federal non-repayable grant programs targeting smaller municipal utilities.
