## 5. Instructions Requiring Adaptation & Repository Observations (Section 5.3)

1. **Subagent interface vs. raw REST `modelVersion` header (Sections 7.1 & 7.2a):** Per PI instruction (`ask_question` sign-off, 2026-11-18), Task A ran via 60 isolated Antigravity `blind_screener_v2` subagents inheriting the active session model (`Gemini 4 Carbon (Medium)`) rather than a standalone `generativelanguage.googleapis.com` API key. Host subagent UUIDs, token counts, SHA-256 hashes, and unedited structured `raw_response` payloads are archived in `gemini_review/raw/`.
2. **Copyright redaction in committed `gemini_review/raw/` files (Section 6 vs. Section 7.2a):** Committing unredacted `raw_request.prompt` strings into `gemini_review/raw/` would commit full-text bodies of 43+ copyrighted papers to a public GitHub repository, violating Section 6 (*"Never commit a paper's PDF or long stretches of its text"*). We therefore kept unredacted request+paper files in local scratch storage and replaced the paper body slot in committed `gemini_review/raw/*.json` files with its character count and `input_sha256`, while keeping `raw_response` 100% unedited.
3. **`study_id` intentionally left empty (`""`):** `A6_TIER3_BLIND_INPUT_FOR_SECOND_AI_2026-11-18.csv` omits `study_id` to preserve blinding. Looking up `study_id` in `extraction_database.csv` or `STUDY_AUDIT_TRAIL.csv` would leak inclusion status; `compare_a6_reviews.py` joins strictly on `record_id` and reads `study_id` from the human sheet, so `study_id` was left `""` across all outputs.
4. **Blinding-design observation on `GEMINI_HANDOFF_BRIEF_2026-11-17.md`:** While `A6_TIER3_BLIND_INPUT_FOR_SECOND_AI_2026-11-18.csv` strips `priority_tier` and `ai_decision`, Section 3 of the hand-off brief explicitly titles Task A *"Blind full-text screening of the 50 tier-3 includes"*. To prevent base-rate anchoring inside the screener, our `blind_screener_v2` prompt withheld any mention of Tier 3 or prior AI inclusion, and the resulting 56.5% exclusion rate among readable papers confirms the screener was not anchored toward agreement. For Task B (Tier 2), mixing tiers or omitting the tier's prior decision from the task header as planned in Section 3 is strongly endorsed.
5. **Google Drive file format note (`RA13F19846E9D`, Order 6):** Drive file `1WXMqQ186hUr3xqke2JErMerq4x9KlNq3` (verified by title in `A6_TIER3_DRIVE_ID_CHECK_2026-11-18.csv`) has a `.pdf` filename on Drive, but its raw bytes (`190.5 KB`) are actually `<!DOCTYPE html>` — the complete Creative Commons SciELO Brazil full-text HTML article in Portuguese (not a landing page). Any downstream PDF tool expecting `%PDF-` magic bytes on `RA13F19846E9D` will fail unless it parses HTML.
6. **64-bit Windows `OverflowError` in repository verification scripts (not edited per Section 6):** `code/analysis/current_figures.py` (line 18) and `code/analysis/validate_schemas.py` (line 41) call `csv.field_size_limit(sys.maxsize)`.
