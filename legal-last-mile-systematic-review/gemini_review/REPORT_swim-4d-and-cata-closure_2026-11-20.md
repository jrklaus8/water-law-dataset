In the multivariable binary logistic regression predicting composite household water security (Table 7, PDF p. 12, adjusting for age, wealth quartiles, water treatment, and toilet type), Household owned/occupied vs Tenants only (Ref) yielded a non-significant adjusted OR = 1.12 (95% CI: 0.62–2.024, p = 0.708; Landlord/tenants occupied OR = 0.975, 95% CI: 0.56–1.699, p = 0.929). Confidence is rated medium because tenure is operationalized as a generic owner-versus-tenant occupancy variable within a sociodemographic predictor battery rather than an analysis of formal land-title or tenancy-law rules.
