## 3. Overall Narrative Summary

Ananga (2015) is a primary mixed-methods PhD dissertation ('partially mixed concurrent equal status design') examining community participation in four NGO-sponsored (SANA) urban informal-settlement water schemes in Kisumu, Kenya (Wandiege, Obunga, Asengo, and Paga), combining a cross-sectional household questionnaire survey (RQ1-RQ2) with 12 focus group discussions, observations, and transect walks (RQ3). Appraised with the Mixed Methods Appraisal Tool (MMAT 2018; Qualitative 1.1-1.5, Quantitative non-randomized 3.1-3.5, Mixed Methods 5.1-5.5), the study has notable strengths in clear research questions (S1-S2), a well-justified mixed-methods rationale (5.1), multi-stakeholder local-language FGD data collection paired with transect walks and observations (1.1-1.2), and a clearly documented seven-stage constant comparative coding and member-checking protocol (1.3). However, per MMAT guidance that overall methodological quality cannot exceed the weakest component, overall risk of bias reflects high methodological concern driven by the quantitative non-randomized component (5.5 = No): (1) the four schemes were purposively chosen with SANA leadership to span high, average, and poor performance, while daytime (8:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m.) interviewing of the eldest household member via SANA/chief-assisted locating yielded a 59%-84% female sample (3.1 = No); (2) exposures and outcomes rely on unvalidated single-item self-reports without physical/microbiological water testing, and RQ1 includes all n = 317 respondents on committee satisfaction despite ~19% primarily using non-scheme water sources (3.2 = No); (3) the RQ1 multivariate logit omits all socioeconomic, demographic, water-source, and scheme fixed-effect confounders despite extreme cross-scheme heterogeneity (e.g., 81.0% satisfaction in Wandiege vs. 16.5% in Paga; 3.4 = No); and (4) sample denominators vary without reconciliation across chapters (400 selected, 360 surveyed, 318 in Table 3.1, 317 in Chapter 4; 91 vs. 71 FGD participants). Finally, because the provided source text was truncated after PDF page 101 of 215 (omitting Section 4.4, Chapters 5-7, and Appendices), qualitative result substantiation (1.4-1.5) and empirical mixed-methods integration (5.2-5.4) are rated 'Can't tell'.
