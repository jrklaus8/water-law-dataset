## 3. Overall Narrative Summary

Study S325 (Silva Rodríguez de San Miguel, 2021) is a secondary systematic literature review framed under PRISMA covering 1990-2019 (65 records identified, 10 duplicates removed, 19 excluded at screening/eligibility, 36 included) on legal, institutional, and governmental approaches to improving rural water supply in the United States. Appraised with AMSTAR 2, overall confidence in the results of the review is Critically Low due to five critical domain failures (Q2 no a priori protocol; Q4 non-comprehensive search distorted by arbitrary journal impact-factor and discipline filtering and lacking Boolean search strings; Q7 no list or study-specific justifications for the 19 excluded studies; Q9 no satisfactory risk-of-bias assessment of included studies; and Q13 failure to account for study quality/RoB in synthesis) alongside failure on every applicable non-critical item (Q1, Q3, Q5, Q6, Q8, Q10, Q14, Q16).
