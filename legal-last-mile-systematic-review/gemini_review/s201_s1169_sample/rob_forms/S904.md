## 3. Overall Narrative Summary

Wahby (2021) is a primary qualitative comparative case study investigating water shortages, infrastructural decay narratives, and everyday socio-technical repair practices (gehood zateya) across two settlements on Cairo's eastern desert periphery: the informal settlement of Izbet ElHaggana (following 2013 legal state pipeline installation) and the elite/middle-class district of ElTagammo' ElKhames in New Cairo. Assessed with the CASP Qualitative Checklist, the study has clear theoretical and empirical aims (Q1), an appropriate qualitative approach (Q2) and comparative two-site design (Q3), clearly articulated findings backed by illustrative vignettes (Q9), and high conceptual value for understanding why formalization and privatization do not displace informal water practices (Q10).
