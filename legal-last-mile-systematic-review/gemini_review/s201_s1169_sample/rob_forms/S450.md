## 3. Overall Narrative Summary

Study S450 (Wang et al., 2025) is a global macro-epidemiological burden-of-disease simulation, time-series forecasting, and demographic decomposition study covering 154 countries from 2000 to 2030. It combines secondary GBD 2021 estimates and WHO/JMP rural toilet coverage series using Comparative Risk Assessment (PAF equations), Bayesian Age-Period-Cohort (BAPC) mortality projections, ARIMA sanitation coverage forecasts, and multi-factor decomposition. No listed risk-of-bias checklist applies (`chosen_tool = "NONE"`): it is neither a randomized trial, a ROBINS-I non-randomized evaluation of an intervention/policy comparator, an individual/ecological analytical cross-sectional or cohort study, a qualitative/mixed-methods study, a systematic review, nor an empirical legal-institutional study. Substantively, despite highlighting 'Lacking Sanitation Governance' in its title, the study measures zero legal, regulatory, or governance variables. Key methodological vulnerabilities include opaque exclusion of 50 GBD / 16 WHO countries for undefined 'outlier' and missing data issues, strong modeling assumptions (imputing overall national age distributions onto rural populations while studying rural aging, and holding sanitation-diarrhea relative risks constant across space and time), an unreported SDI fixed-effect linear regression mentioned in Methods, and internal reporting errors (confusing thousands and millions in death counts on pp. 6–7 and referencing 'air quality' in the Figure 5 caption on p. 7).
