## 3. Overall Narrative Summary

Study S294 (Quattrochi et al., 2025) is a large, policy-embedded cluster-randomized controlled trial evaluating the 3.6-year sustainability and health/institutional impacts of the DRC's national community-driven Healthy Villages WASH program across 121 spatial village clusters (332 villages; 3,283 households) in four provinces. Appraised with the RoB 2 cluster-randomized trials variant, the overall risk of bias is judged as Some concerns (with Low risk in D2 and Some concerns in D1a, D1b, D3, D4, and D5). Major methodological strengths include centralized stratified block randomization in Stata with >=2.5 km (>=1 km in South Kivu) spatial buffers to limit spillovers, intention-to-treat estimation with stratum fixed effects and cluster-robust standard errors, 99.1% village retention (329/332 villages) at 3.6 years, data collection by an independent research organization (IHfRA) with 15% village re-interviews and supervisor anthropometry re-checks, public availability of microdata and replication code, and objective measurement of child linear growth, E. coli/thermotolerant coliform water quality, water point functionality, and structured pre-interview handwashing observations. Key limitations driving Some concerns are: (1) absence of a pre-randomization baseline survey and post-randomization dropping of 7 villages (6 randomly for operational targets and 1 control village due to a coding error) (D1a); (2) post-randomization, post-intervention household sampling and recruitment by unmasked enumerators, though mitigated by random-walk selection and a >=4-year residency criterion (D1b); (3) 11%-14% longitudinal household attrition/replacement and ~10% missing child LAZ among eligible households without baseline data to verify non-differential out-migration (D3); (4) unmasked self-reporting for primary diarrhea, the WASH governance index, improved water/sanitation use, and governance perceptions, where reactivity is underscored by +9 pp self-reported handwashing alongside null structured handwashing observation and null household E. coli differences (D4); and (5) retrospective trial registration with the 3.6-year pre-analysis plan posted in April 2023 at the end of fieldwork, albeit before principal investigators inspected the data (D5).
