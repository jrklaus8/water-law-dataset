## 3. Overall Narrative Summary

S525 (Kachenje, 2025) is a primary qualitative comparative case study examining institutional coordination challenges across public, private, and community-based urban water supply schemes in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania, drawing on coordination, institutional, and network theories. Appraised with the CASP Qualitative Checklist, the study raises high methodological concern (4 'Yes', 2 'No', and 3 'Can't tell' ratings across Q1-Q9, plus constrained empirical value on Q10). While the research aim (Q1), qualitative approach (Q2), comparative three-scheme case design (Q3), and thematic summary of five coordination barriers (Q9) are clearly articulated, fundamental reporting and evidentiary omissions undermine internal credibility.
