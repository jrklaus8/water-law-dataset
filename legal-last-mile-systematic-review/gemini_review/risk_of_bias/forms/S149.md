## 3. Overall Narrative Summary

Sethy and Sahoo (2026) report a convergent parallel mixed-methods study of gendered WASH inequalities across four purposively selected districts (16 villages, 400 surveyed households, 16 FGDs, 32 KIIs, and VWSC/Gram Sabha observations) in rural Odisha, India, supplemented by an Alkire–Foster Gender–WASH Inequality Index (GWII) and linear-programming budget optimization. Appraised with the Mixed Methods Appraisal Tool (MMAT 2018, using the quantitative non-randomized block 3.1–3.5 alongside qualitative 1.1–1.5 and mixed-methods 5.1–5.5), the study exhibits high methodological concern.
