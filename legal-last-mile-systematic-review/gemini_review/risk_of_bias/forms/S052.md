## 3. Overall Narrative Summary

Muoghalu, Semiyaga, and Manga (2023) conducted a PRISMA-guided systematic review of 37 publications (2002-2021) examining faecal sludge emptying technology choices, operator challenges, and improvement initiatives across Sub-Saharan Africa, South Asia, and Southeast Asia. Appraised under AMSTAR 2, overall confidence in the review's results is Critically Low due to five critical flaws (Q2, Q4, Q7, Q9, and Q13) alongside multiple non-critical weaknesses (Q3, Q5, Q6, Q8, Q10, and Q14). Specifically, the authors reported no pre-registered or a priori protocol (Q2); did not justify restricting searches to English-language literature despite covering Francophone/Lusophone Africa and Asia, nor describe systematic grey-literature or reference-list searching (Q4); provided neither a bibliography nor aggregate exclusion reasons for the 94 excluded full-text articles (Q7); conducted no risk-of-bias or quality appraisal of the 37 included studies (Q9); and consequently did not account for primary-study risk of bias when synthesizing results via unadjusted publication vote-counting (Q13). In addition, duplicate independent screening and data extraction were not reported (Q5, Q6), no master table of the 37 included studies was presented in the main text (Q8), grey literature/theses/reports were cited despite a stated peer-reviewed journal restriction (Q3), and minor numerical inconsistencies occurred between the Abstract (health challenges n = 12) and Section 3.4.2 (n = 14).
