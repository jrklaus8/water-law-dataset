## 3. Overall Narrative Summary

Thapa, Farid, and Prevost (2022) conduct a sequential explanatory mixed-methods study of 10-year rural water system sustainability under Indonesia's PAMSIMAS program, combining Poisson and logit regressions on project MIS panel data (2014–2018) merged with PODES village data (N = 4,218 villages in Appendix C) with week-long qualitative fieldwork in seven outlier villages (three positive deviance and four negative deviance cases). Appraised with the MMAT (2018), mixed-methods rationale and integration (items 5.1–5.4) are strong: quantitative regressions motivate purposive outlier selection, and multi-stakeholder qualitative fieldwork (FGDs, participant observation, and key informant interviews) persuasively illuminates how collaborative vs. competitive/apathetic village head–water user group relations shape socialization quality, leadership decision space, and sustained user tariff compliance. However, per MMAT guidance that overall methodological quality cannot exceed the weakest component, overall concern is high (low quality in the quantitative component, criterion 5.5 = No). Specifically, the quantitative non-randomized component suffers from (1) an unexplained sample drop from 6,507 Phase I villages (minus ~250 data errors, PDF p. 6) to 4,218 villages in Appendix C (PDF pp. 26–28); (2) facilitator-reported subjective functionality outcomes vulnerable to positive overreporting; (3) operationalizing participation via raw log attendance headcount without adjusting for village population size; (4) controlling for contemporaneous tariff collection (an endogenous post-inception mediator) while omitting district/island fixed effects and other baseline confounders; and (5) non-uniform intervention fidelity. In the qualitative component, data collection and empirical substantiation are strong, but analytic coding procedures, researcher reflexivity (World Bank staff studying a World Bank program), substantive coverage of Banyumas District, and the village summary table promised in Footnote 18 (missing from Appendix B) are omitted.
