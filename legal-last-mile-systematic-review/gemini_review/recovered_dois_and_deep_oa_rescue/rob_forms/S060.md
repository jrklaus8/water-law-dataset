## 3. Overall Narrative Summary

This analytical cross-sectional household survey (N=379) in Matungulu Sub-County, Machakos County, Kenya exhibits high methodological concern across all eight JBI appraisal domains. While the authors state that systematic sampling was applied across two sub-locations, they do not identify the selected sub-locations, eligibility criteria, sampling frame, survey dates, response rate or any demographic/socioeconomic characteristics of the 379 respondents. Both the policy exposure ('Integration of RHT in County Development Agenda') and the outcome ('Impact on Household Livelihoods') are derived from unvalidated self-report questionnaire scores collected simultaneously from the same respondents, creating severe common-method bias; the outcome construct is never defined or summarized descriptively, and perceived county empowerment (95% 'Yes' in Table 1) contradicts respondents' own reports that county initiatives accounted for 0.3% of community RHT integration (Figure 2) and 2% of capital financing (Figure 3). Furthermore, no confounding factors were identified or controlled for in the bivariate correlation and single-predictor linear regression, multiple predictors specified in Methods (X1, X2) were omitted from the reported regression results, and several numerical reporting errors appear in the text and figures.
