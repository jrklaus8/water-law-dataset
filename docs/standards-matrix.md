# Standards and requirements matrix

**What this is.** A list of the institutional, legal and community requirements that appear to apply to this project, with what the repository shows about each. It was prepared by an AI assistant (Claude Code) on 2026-10-27 as part of the self-assessment in [`audit-report.md`](audit-report.md). **It is not an official university review, a certification, an ethics approval or a legal opinion**, and it does not claim that the project complies with any policy, law or licence.

**How to read the "source" column.** The Erasmus University Rotterdam (EUR) and other pages named below were found through a web search during the audit. The pages themselves could not be opened from the audit environment (a direct fetch of eur.nl failed), so what is written here about their content comes from search-result summaries, not from reading the policies. Every URL is shown as the search returned it and may have moved. The date accessed is therefore the date of the search, not of a reading. **Nothing marked "awaiting confirmation" has been checked against the real text.** The first action for each is to read the page.

**Status words** (used exactly as defined here):

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| verified | the requirement's text was read and the repository evidence was checked against it |
| partially verified | the repository evidence was checked, but the requirement text was not read in full, or only part of the requirement is covered |
| not met | the repository evidence shows the requirement is not satisfied |
| not applicable | the requirement does not apply, with the reason given |
| awaiting confirmation | whether the requirement applies or is satisfied cannot be decided from the repository; someone must confirm |

**Applicability.** The task description names EUR and the Erasmus School of Law (ESL) as the relevant institution. The repository itself records no affiliation, funder, ethics decision or data steward, so applicability below rests on that statement and must be confirmed by the researcher.

## E. Summary

- No row is marked verified for compliance. The only "verified" entry (S-21) is verified for schema validity of the citation files and nothing else. One row is **not met** (S-19, prospective registration, which the review discloses). The community-reporting rows (S-16 to S-20) and the first rows of the institutional list are **partially verified**: the repository holds documentation that addresses the requirement, but the requirement text was not read and compared line by line. - There is deliberately no score or percentage: the rows are not equal in weight, and "awaiting confirmation" means the question is open, not that the answer is bad.
- Almost every institutional row needs a person at EUR to confirm. The researcher can start by reading the pages listed and by asking the data steward, the ethics committee and the privacy contact the questions in [`audit-report.md`](audit-report.md), section H.
